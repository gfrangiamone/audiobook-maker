Why Convert EPUB to Audiobook?

EPUB is the most popular ebook format, used by Apple Books, Google Play Books, Kobo, and most digital libraries. Converting EPUB to audiobook lets you listen to your ebooks while commuting, exercising, or doing chores. Modern AI text-to-speech voices sound remarkably natural — far better than robotic screen readers.

With Audiobook Maker, you can convert any DRM-free EPUB to MP3 or M4B audiobook for free, directly in your browser. No software to install, no account required.

How to Convert EPUB to Audiobook — Step by Step

  1. Upload your EPUB file — Drag and drop or click to select. The tool automatically extracts chapters and metadata (title, author, cover).
  2. Choose your TTS voice — Select from 400+ neural AI voices across 50+ languages. Preview a short sample before committing to the full conversion.
  3. Select chapters — Pick which chapters to include. Skip the table of contents, copyright pages, or any sections you don't want narrated.
  4. Pick output format — Choose MP3 (single file or ZIP per chapter), M4B (single file with embedded chapters and cover art — ideal for Apple Books and audiobook players), or Podcast RSS (private podcast feed).
  5. Click "Generate" — The TTS engine narrates each chapter. You'll get a progress bar and optional email notification when done.
  6. Download and listen — Download your audiobook and start listening on any device.

EPUB to MP3 vs EPUB to M4B: Which Format Should You Choose?

MP3 is universally compatible — every phone, tablet, and computer plays MP3 files. Choose MP3 if you want maximum compatibility or plan to listen on multiple devices.

M4B is the professional audiobook format. It's a single file that contains all chapters as navigation markers, plus embedded cover art and metadata (author, title, genre). M4B files are supported by Apple Books, Audible, and most dedicated audiobook apps. Learn more about the M4B format →

Tips for the Best EPUB to Audiobook Experience

  • Remove DRM first: Commercial ebooks from Kindle, Apple Books, or Kobo often have DRM protection. You'll need to remove it before conversion (for personal use only, where legally permitted).
  • Clean the text: Some EPUBs have formatting artifacts (page numbers, headers, footnotes). Audiobook Maker's optional AI text optimization can clean these up automatically.
  • Preview before full generation: Always generate the free preview first to check voice quality and pacing.
  • Use the chapter selector: Skip front matter (TOC, preface) and back matter (index, ads) for a cleaner listening experience.
  • Choose M4B for long books: The M4B format keeps everything organized in one file with chapter navigation — much better than juggling multiple MP3 files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free to convert EPUB to audiobook?

Yes. Audiobook Maker is open-source software (AGPL-3.0). TTS conversion uses Microsoft Edge TTS which is free with no usage limits. The optional AI text optimization (DeepSeek LLM) has a small cost above a free threshold.

Can I convert Kindle books to audiobooks?

Kindle books use Amazon's proprietary AZW/KFX format with DRM. You'll need to remove the DRM and convert to EPUB first using a tool like Calibre, then upload the EPUB to Audiobook Maker.

How long does EPUB to audiobook conversion take?

Approximately 2-3 minutes per chapter (varies by chapter length and server load). A typical 300-page book (~20 chapters) takes about 40-60 minutes. You'll receive an email notification when it's done.

What languages are supported?

50+ languages including English, Italian, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, and many more. Each language has multiple voice options.

Does Audiobook Maker work on mobile?

Yes. The web app works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, or phone. However, for large EPUB files, a desktop browser is recommended for faster upload and processing.
