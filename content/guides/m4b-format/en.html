What Is an M4B File?

An M4B file is an audiobook: AAC audio inside an MPEG-4 container, with chapter markers, cover art and a saved listening position. You can open it with Apple Books on iPhone and Mac, with VLC on Windows and Linux, and with any audiobook app on Android.

M4B (MPEG-4 Audiobook) belongs to the same family as MP4 video and M4A music files. What makes it an audiobook format is a set of features built for long listening:

  • Chapter markers: Embedded navigation points let you jump between chapters
  • Cover art: The book cover is embedded in the file metadata
  • Bookmarks: Audiobook players remember your listening position (even across devices with iCloud)
  • Metadata: Title, author, narrator, genre, and publication date are all stored in the file
  • Variable speed: Players can speed up or slow down playback without changing pitch

M4B is the format used by Apple Books and by most dedicated audiobook apps on iOS and Android. Audible's AAX files are built on the same container, but they are DRM-protected and only play in Audible's apps.

Bottom line: Choose M4B if you use Apple Books or a dedicated audiobook app. Choose MP3 if you need to play the file on a basic MP3 player or car stereo that doesn't support M4B.

How to Open an M4B File

iPhone and iPad

  1. Save the M4B file to the Files app (or receive it via AirDrop).
  2. Tap the file, then the share icon, and choose Books. It appears under Audiobooks with its chapters.
  3. Prefer a dedicated player? BookPlayer (free and open source) and Bound import M4B files directly from Files.

Mac

Double-click the M4B file: it opens in Apple Books, which keeps your position and syncs it with your iPhone through iCloud. VLC and IINA also play it, with chapters in the playback menu.

Windows

Windows has no built-in audiobook app. Use VLC media player (free): open the file and pick a chapter from Playback → Chapter. foobar2000 and MusicBee also read M4B, and iTunes for Windows files it under Audiobooks. If an app does not recognise the file, rename the extension from .m4b to .m4a: the content is the same, only some players may then ignore the chapters.

Android

Install an audiobook player: Smart AudioBook Player, Listen Audiobook Player or the open-source Voice. Copy the M4B to your phone (a folder such as Audiobooks), point the app at that folder and the chapters appear automatically. VLC for Android works too.

Linux

VLC, mpv or Cozy, a GTK audiobook player that remembers your position.

Car and home server

Most car stereos read MP3 but not M4B from a USB stick: convert the file (see below) or play it from your phone via CarPlay or Android Auto with one of the apps above. To stream your library at home, Audiobookshelf and Plex (with the Audnexus agent) handle M4B with chapters.

How to Convert M4B to MP3 (and MP3 to M4B)

M4B to MP3. The free command-line tool ffmpeg converts the whole book in one step:

ffmpeg -i book.m4b -vn -c:a libmp3lame -b:a 64k book.mp3

64 kbps is plenty for a spoken voice. Most MP3 players ignore chapter markers, so if you need to jump between chapters it is better to have one MP3 per chapter: m4b-tool split cuts the M4B at its chapter marks.

MP3 to M4B. To merge a folder of MP3 files into a single M4B with one chapter per file, use m4b-tool merge "book-folder/" --output-file="book.m4b" or the open-source AudioBookConverter app (Windows, Mac, Linux).

Starting from an ebook? You do not need any of these tools: Audiobook Maker reads the EPUB or PDF and gives you M4B with chapters, a single MP3, or a ZIP with one MP3 per chapter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between M4A and M4B?

None in the audio itself: both are AAC in an MPEG-4 container and both can hold chapters. The .m4b extension tells players the file is an audiobook, so they remember your position and file it under Audiobooks instead of Music. Renaming one extension to the other does not damage the file.

Why won't my M4B file open?

Usually for one of three reasons: the app does not know the .m4b extension (try VLC, or rename it to .m4a), the download was interrupted (the file is smaller than expected or stops early: download it again), or the file is DRM-protected, like Audible AAX files and some older iTunes Store purchases, which only play in the store's own app.

Is M4B the same as Audible's AAX?

They share the MPEG-4 container, but AAX is encrypted with Audible's DRM and needs an authorised Audible app. The M4B files created with Audiobook Maker have no DRM and play in any compatible player.

Can I split an M4B into chapters?

Yes. Tools like m4b-tool or ffmpeg can split M4B files at chapter markers. Audiobook Maker can also output individual MP3 files per chapter if you prefer separate files.

What audiobook apps support M4B?

Apple Books (iOS/Mac), BookPlayer and Bound (iOS), Smart AudioBook Player, Listen Audiobook Player and Voice (Android), VLC (all platforms), Audiobookshelf, and Plex with the Audnexus agent.

What bitrate should I use for M4B audiobooks?

For spoken word, 48–64 kbps AAC is the usual choice: a narrated voice sounds clean at that rate and a 10-hour book takes about 200–300 MB. Audiobook Maker keeps the bitrate of the narration, 48 kbps with the free voices.
