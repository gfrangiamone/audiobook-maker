What is a voucher?

A voucher (or coupon) is prepaid credit in euros that you spend on the PREMIUM services of Audiobook Maker. It has a code such as ABCD-EFGH-JKLM, sometimes with a prefix (PROMO-, GIFT-, SUB-), and it is linked to the email address it was issued to.

You don't need a voucher to create an audiobook: with the Standard voices the conversion is free. A voucher is only used when you choose a paid service.

What you can pay for with a voucher

  • PREMIUM voices: the models offered in the Model selector of the PREMIUM voices.
  • AI text optimization: required only for books above the free threshold.
  • AI book translation.
  • Voice sampling: an audiobook read with your own voice (see the voice cloning guide).

Each voucher has its own list of services, written in the email that contains the code. When a voucher covers a voice model, it also covers the AI text optimization paid together with that audiobook.

Refund vouchers

If a job you paid for with PayPal fails, the amount comes back as a voucher sent by email. If you paid with a voucher, the amount simply goes back onto the same code: no new code and no email. If you cancel a PREMIUM generation yourself, the cost of the part already generated is kept and the rest is refunded.

Donation vouchers

Donations of €5 or more receive a voucher of the same value, sent by email to the address linked to the donation. Thank you: donations help cover running costs and new features.

Subscription vouchers

Every paid month of a subscription includes a PREMIUM voucher valid for 30 days. It belongs to your account: when you pay for a PREMIUM service while signed in, it is offered automatically and you don't need to copy the code. You also see it, with its balance, on your account page.

Donation vouchers and voice models

Among the PREMIUM voice models, a donation voucher pays only for Audiobook Maker (VoxCPM2), sampled voices included. The same applies to subscription vouchers. Before you start, open the Model selector of the PREMIUM voices and choose Audiobook Maker (VoxCPM2).

With any other model, the payment window shows “This voucher is not valid for the selected service”. Switch the model to Audiobook Maker (VoxCPM2), or pay for that model with PayPal. AI text optimization, book translation and voice sampling can always be paid with a donation voucher.

How to use a voucher, step by step

  1. Prepare the book as usual: upload it, choose the chapters and the PREMIUM voice or service.
  2. Start the generation, the optimization or the translation: the payment window opens with the total.
  3. Choose the “Use a voucher” tab.
  4. Enter the voucher code and the email associated with the voucher, the one that received it.
  5. The voucher is checked automatically and the available balance appears. Confirm and the job starts.

Signed in to your account? The payment window proposes the voucher of your account that covers the payment, already filled in.

Balance, expiry and refunds

  • Reusable: if the job costs less than the balance, the rest stays on the code until it expires. Next time, enter the same code.
  • One voucher per payment: the balance must cover the whole total. A voucher can't be combined with PayPal or with another voucher. If the balance isn't enough, select fewer chapters or pay with PayPal.
  • Expiry: refund vouchers last 180 days, subscription vouchers 30 days; for the others, the date is in the email.
  • If something goes wrong: when a job paid with a voucher fails, the amount returns to the same voucher automatically.

Messages in the payment window

  • Voucher not found: check the code. Apart from the prefix, codes never contain the letters O and I or the digits 0 and 1.
  • Email mismatch: enter the email the voucher was sent to.
  • This voucher is not valid for the selected service: the voucher doesn't include that service or voice model. See the list in the voucher email.
  • Insufficient balance: the total is higher than the balance.
  • Voucher expired or Voucher already used: the voucher can no longer be spent.
  • This voucher belongs to another account: sign in with the account the subscription voucher belongs to.
  • Too many verification attempts: for security there is a limit on checks. Wait the time shown and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a voucher to convert a book for free?

No. The Standard voices are free and need neither a voucher nor an account. Vouchers are only for PREMIUM services.

How do I check how much is left on my voucher?

Enter it in the “Use a voucher” tab: the available balance appears as soon as it is checked. Subscription vouchers are also shown, with balance and expiry, on your account page.

Which voice models can I use with a donation voucher?

Among the PREMIUM voice models, only Audiobook Maker (VoxCPM2), sampled voices included. It also pays for AI text optimization, book translation and voice sampling.

I lost the email with the code. What can I do?

Search your mailbox for “Audiobook Maker”, spam folder included. If you can't find it, write to us from the support page using the email the voucher was issued to.

Can someone else use my voucher?

Whoever uses it must enter both the code and the email it was issued to. Subscription vouchers work only in the account they belong to.
